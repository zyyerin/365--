A documentation of daily coding practices: building a visual language through programming. Technology used to realize a design has significant impact on the look of the result for its embedded presets. Programming makes it possible to construct design with logics and semantics — a square becomes a collection of its size, position, fill, stroke, and movements. Click any tile to run the sketch.
